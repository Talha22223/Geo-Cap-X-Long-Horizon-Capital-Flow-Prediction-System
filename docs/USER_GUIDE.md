# GeoCap-X User & Administrator Guide

## 1. Executive Summary
GeoCap-X provides institutional analysts, portfolio managers, and risk officers with predictive foresight on global capital flow rotations across 6-month to 5-year horizons.

---

## 2. Platform Modules & Navigation

### 📊 Executive Dashboard (`/dashboard`)
- High-level metric summary of active capital flow predictions, systemic risk index, high-severity event counts, and macro signals.
- Live capital flow forecast feed with instant filtering by Region, Asset Class, and Time Horizon.

### 🌐 Capital Flow Visualizer (`/flows`)
- Interactive geographic heatmaps and sankey diagrams illustrating cross-border capital rotations.
- Detailed decomposition of Inflow vs. Outflow vectors.

### 🔗 Event Chain Causality Graph (`/events`)
- Network graph visualizer rendering geopolitical propagation paths.
- Centrality metric breakdowns (PageRank, Betweenness, Closeness) highlighting systemic catalyst nodes.

### 📉 Technical Market Analysis (`/technical`)
- Overlay of traditional technical indicators (RSI, MACD, Moving Averages) against AI capital flow predictions for market confirmation.

### 🎯 Predictions Deep-Dive (`/predictions`)
- Complete explainability view displaying:
  - Confidence metric gauge (Extraction, Classification, Prediction, Overall).
  - Mathematical & qualitative reasoning summary.
  - Supporting raw events and historical analog comparisons.
  - Alternative scenario probabilities (Bull, Bear, Base cases).

### ⚙️ Organization & Administration (`/settings` & `/admin`)
- Workgroup member management, RBAC permission roles, API Key provisioning, and SaaS audit trail logs.
